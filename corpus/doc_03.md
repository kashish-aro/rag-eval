# Holiday Calendar and Office Closures

Meridian observes 11 paid company holidays each year for United States employees. Lisbon employees observe the Portuguese public holiday calendar, which is published separately by the People team each January.

## Observed Holidays

New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. Two floating holidays are granted to each employee annually and may be taken on any day with manager notice.

Floating holidays do not carry over into the next calendar year.

## Winter Closure

Both offices close from December 24 through January 1 inclusive. The winter closure does not count against an employee's PTO balance and is not deducted from accrued days.

Employees on the on-call rotation during the winter closure receive two additional floating holidays to be used in the following quarter.

## Observance Rules

When a company holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.
