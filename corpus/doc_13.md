# Atlas: Product Overview

Atlas is Meridian's workflow automation platform. It launched in 2021 and is the company's primary revenue product.

## What It Does

Atlas lets customers define workflows that connect internal systems without writing integration code. A workflow is a sequence of steps, each of which reads from or writes to a connected system. Workflows are triggered by schedule, by webhook, or manually.

## Tiers

Atlas is sold in three tiers: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. Tiers differ in workflow limits, API rate limits, support response times, and available connectors.

Starter supports up to 25 active workflows. Growth supports up to 250. Enterprise has no workflow limit.

## Connectors

Atlas ships 60 first-party connectors. Starter customers can use 10 connectors, Growth can use all 60, and Enterprise customers can additionally build private connectors using the Atlas SDK.

## Regions

Atlas runs in three regions: us-central, eu-west, and ap-southeast. Customers select a region at account creation. Region cannot be changed after creation without a migration request to support.

## Audit Log

All tiers include an audit log of workflow executions. Retention of audit log data follows the company data retention policy.
