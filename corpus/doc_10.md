# Information Security Policy

## Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required on every Meridian account without exception. Hardware security keys are required for all Engineering and Operations staff. Software authenticators are permitted for other organizations.

The company password manager is mandatory. Storing credentials in browsers, notes applications, source code, or spreadsheets is prohibited.

## Devices

All company laptops run full-disk encryption and the company endpoint agent. Laptops lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may not access production systems or customer data.

## Training

Security training is required at onboarding and annually thereafter. The annual refresh is assigned each February and must be completed within 30 days of assignment. Access to production is suspended if training is more than 14 days overdue.

## Reporting

Suspected phishing is reported using the Report Phishing button in the mail client, which routes to the Security team. Do not forward suspected phishing manually.

Suspected security incidents involving customer data must be reported to the Security team immediately and are handled under the incident response runbook.
