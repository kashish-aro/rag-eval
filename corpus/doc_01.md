# Meridian Systems: Company Overview

Meridian Systems was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Denver, Colorado. As of the most recent all-hands, Meridian employs 340 people across four organizations: Engineering, Product, Go-to-Market, and Operations.

## Organizations

Engineering is the largest org at roughly 140 people, led by CTO Dara Whitfield. It is divided into four groups: Platform, Atlas Product Engineering, Infrastructure, and Security.

Product includes product management, design, and technical writing, and reports to Chief Product Officer Nils Ostrander.

Go-to-Market covers sales, solutions engineering, marketing, and customer support.

Operations covers finance, people, legal, and IT.

## Offices

Meridian maintains two offices: the Denver headquarters and a smaller engineering site in Lisbon, Portugal, opened in 2023. Roughly 40 percent of employees are fully remote.

## Cadence

Company all-hands is held on the first Thursday of each month. Each organization runs its own weekly staff meeting. Meridian operates on a quarterly planning cycle, with planning weeks falling in the last two weeks of March, June, September, and December.
