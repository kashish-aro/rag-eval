# Remote Work and Equipment

## In-Office Expectations

Employees assigned to the Denver or Lisbon offices are expected on site three days per week: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday are optional in-office days.

Fully remote employees have no in-office requirement but are expected to travel to Denver twice per year for team onsites. Onsite travel is booked under the standard travel policy.

## Home Office Stipend

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $1,200, available beginning on their start date and expiring if unused after 90 days. The stipend may be spent on desks, chairs, monitors, lighting, and other home office equipment.

A refresh stipend of $400 is granted every two years on the employee's start date anniversary.

Stipend purchases are submitted through Ledger and are tagged with the Home Office category. Receipts are required for all stipend purchases regardless of amount.

## Laptops

Every employee is issued a company laptop. Engineers receive a 16-inch laptop with 64 GB of memory by default. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Personal devices may not be used to access production systems.
