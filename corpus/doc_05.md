# Travel Booking and Per Diem

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through Voyager, Meridian's travel portal. Travel booked outside Voyager is reimbursed only with a written exception from a director or above.

Flights must be booked at least 14 days before departure. Any flight with a fare over $800 requires director approval before booking, regardless of how far in advance it is booked.

Economy class is the default for all flights. Premium economy is permitted on flights with a scheduled duration over six hours. Business class requires VP approval.

## Per Diem

Meridian pays a flat per diem for meals and incidentals rather than reimbursing individual meal receipts on travel days. The domestic per diem is $65 per day. The international per diem is $95 per day.

Per diem is paid for full travel days. Departure and return days are paid at 75 percent of the standard rate.

Per diem does not cover ground transportation, which is reimbursed separately with receipts under the expense policy.

## Hotels

Hotel bookings should not exceed $300 per night in standard markets or $425 per night in high-cost markets. The current high-cost market list is maintained in Voyager.
