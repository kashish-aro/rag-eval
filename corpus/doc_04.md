# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for any expense over $75. Expenses of $75 or less may be submitted without a receipt but still require a business justification in the expense description.

## Submission

Expenses must be submitted through Ledger, Meridian's expense system, within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. Expenses submitted after 30 days require a written exception from a director or above and are not guaranteed to be reimbursed.

## Approval and Payment

Every expense report routes to the submitter's direct manager for approval. Reports totaling more than $2,500 route to the manager and then to the org's finance partner. Approved reimbursements are paid on the 15th of the month following approval.

## Non-Reimbursable

The following are not reimbursable: alcohol purchased outside of a client meal, traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, flight upgrades not approved in advance, and hotel minibar charges.

## Client Meals

Client meals require the names of all attendees and the company they represent to be listed in the expense description. There is no per-head cap on client meals, but reports over $2,500 follow the finance routing above.
