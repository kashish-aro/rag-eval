# Support Ticket Priorities and Response Targets

## Priorities

P1: production down or data loss for the customer. P2: major functionality impaired with no workaround. P3: functionality impaired with a workaround available. P4: question, documentation issue, or feature request.

## First Response Targets

Standard targets apply to Starter and Growth customers. P1 receives a first response within 1 hour. P2 within 4 business hours. P3 within 2 business days. P4 within 5 business days.

Enterprise customers receive a first response to P1 within 30 minutes and to P2 within 2 business hours. P3 and P4 targets are the same across all tiers.

## Business Hours

Business hours are 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM in the customer's selected support region, Monday through Friday, excluding Meridian holidays. P1 targets apply 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for all tiers.

## Escalation

A P1 open for more than 2 hours without resolution is escalated to the support manager and, if the cause is a platform issue, raised as an incident under the incident response runbook.

Customers may request a priority increase. Support owns the final priority assignment.
