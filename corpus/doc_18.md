# Data Retention and Deletion

## Customer Data

Customer workflow data and configuration are retained for 90 days after an account is cancelled. At the end of the 90-day window the data is deleted permanently and cannot be recovered. Customers may request earlier deletion.

Reactivating an account within the 90-day window restores all data.

## Audit and Execution Logs

Workflow execution logs are retained for 30 days on Starter and Growth, and 180 days on Enterprise. Audit logs of account and key changes are retained for one year on all tiers.

## Backups

Production backups are retained for one year. Backups are encrypted at rest and stored in the same region as the source account data.

Backups are excluded from individual deletion requests. Data in backups ages out on the standard one-year schedule.

## Deletion Requests

A verified customer deletion request is completed within 30 days of receipt. The Security team confirms completion in writing.

## Internal Data

Employee records are retained for seven years after departure as required by Meridian's records policy. Application materials from candidates who are not hired are retained for one year.
