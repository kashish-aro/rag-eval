# Engineering Interview Process

Meridian runs a four-stage process for engineering candidates.

## Stage One: Recruiter Screen

A 30-minute conversation covering background, motivation, and logistics.

## Stage Two: Technical Screen

A 60-minute technical conversation with an engineer, held over video. The engineer submits written feedback within 24 hours.

## Stage Three: Take-Home

Candidates receive a take-home exercise scoped to four hours of work and have five calendar days to return it. Candidates who prefer not to complete a take-home may substitute a second 60-minute technical screen with a different engineer.

Take-homes are reviewed by two engineers independently. Reviewers do not see each other's scores before submitting their own.

## Stage Four: Onsite

Three panels: a system design panel, a code review panel, and a values panel. Each panel runs 60 minutes.

## Debrief

The hiring panel debriefs within 48 hours of the onsite. A hire decision requires unanimous agreement from all onsite interviewers. The hiring manager may not override a no-hire vote.

Candidates receive a decision within five business days of the onsite regardless of outcome.
