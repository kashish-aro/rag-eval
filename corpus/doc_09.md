# Performance Review Cycle

## Frequency

Meridian runs formal performance reviews twice per year. The spring cycle concludes in March and the fall cycle concludes in September.

## Timeline

Self-reviews open six weeks before the cycle close and are due two weeks before close. Manager reviews are written after self-reviews are submitted. Calibration sessions are held in the final week.

## Ratings

Employees are rated on a five-point scale: 1 Not Meeting, 2 Partially Meeting, 3 Meeting, 4 Exceeding, 5 Outstanding. A rating of 3 is the expected outcome for an employee performing well in their role.

Ratings of 5 require calibration approval at the org level. No more than 10 percent of an organization may receive a 5 in a single cycle.

## Peer Feedback

Each employee names three peer reviewers. Managers may add up to two additional reviewers. Peer feedback is shared with the employee in summarized form and is not attributed.

## Compensation

Compensation adjustments are decided in the spring cycle only. The fall cycle is developmental and does not affect compensation. Promotions may be granted in either cycle.
