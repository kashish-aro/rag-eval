# Paid Time Off Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off during their first year of employment, accruing at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Beginning in the third year of employment, the annual accrual increases to 20 days, or 1.67 days per month.

PTO begins accruing on the first day of employment. New employees may not take PTO during their first 60 days except in cases of emergency approved by their manager.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Days beyond the 5-day cap are forfeited on December 31 and are not paid out.

## Requests

PTO must be requested through Compass, Meridian's internal people system, at least 14 days in advance for any absence of three days or longer. Shorter absences require 48 hours notice. PTO is taken in half-day increments at minimum.

Manager approval is required for all requests. Managers are expected to respond to a PTO request within three business days.

## Sick Time

Sick time is tracked separately from PTO and is not capped. Sick time does not accrue and does not carry over.
