# On-Call Rotation and Escalation

## Rotation

On-call rotations run one week, beginning and ending at 10:00 AM Mountain Time on Wednesdays. Each rotation has a primary and a secondary responder. The outgoing primary runs a handoff with the incoming primary at the Wednesday transition, covering open incidents and known risks.

Engineers on the Platform, Atlas Product Engineering, and Infrastructure groups participate. Engineers are not scheduled on-call during their first 90 days.

## Escalation Path

If the primary does not acknowledge a page within 10 minutes, the page automatically escalates to the secondary. If the secondary does not acknowledge within a further 10 minutes, the page escalates to the group's engineering manager.

Any responder may escalate manually at any time without justification. Escalating early is explicitly encouraged and is never treated as a negative signal.

## Compensation

On-call weeks are compensated with a $500 stipend per week. Engineers who are paged between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM may take the following morning off without using PTO.

## Swaps

Rotation swaps are arranged directly between engineers and recorded in the scheduling tool at least 48 hours in advance.
