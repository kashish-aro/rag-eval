# New Hire Onboarding: Week One

## Before Day One

IT ships the laptop to arrive two business days before the start date. The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the same team and posts an introduction in the team channel.

## Day One

The new hire completes account setup, enrolls in multi-factor authentication, and installs the company password manager. The People team runs a 90-minute orientation session at 10:00 AM Mountain Time.

## Day Two and Three

The new hire meets their buddy for a codebase walkthrough and completes the required security training. Security training must be completed by end of day three. Access to production systems is not granted until it is complete.

## Day Four and Five

The new hire is expected to open and merge one small pull request by end of week one. Teams are expected to have a starter issue tagged and ready before the start date. The goal of the first pull request is to exercise the full path from local development to production deploy, not to deliver meaningful scope.

## First Thirty Days

The hiring manager holds a 30-day check-in. Onboarding buddies remain assigned for the full 30 days.
